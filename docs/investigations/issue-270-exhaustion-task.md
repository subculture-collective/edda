## Bounded outcome

Implement only the shared Exhaustion condition of PDF181: one authoritative per-creature level ledger, its two derived penalties, death at level 6 and the ending at level 0. No published task owns these. Existing reducers keep their own triggers and are not copied: #428 completed Long Rest, #623 Greater Restoration and #1001 Tireless each remove one level through this ledger's single reduce-one output and never keep a second counter. Sources of Exhaustion (dehydration, malnutrition, suffocation, travel, temperature, contagions, items and stat blocks) stay with their own owners; this task admits a level only from a trusted, source-bound gain event and enables no such source in production.

Levels (PDF179, 181): Exhaustion is the one condition that stacks with itself. Each trusted receipt of the condition adds exactly 1 level and records its source, receipt and order. Valid stored levels are 1–5; level 0 means the condition is absent. At level 6 the creature dies: commit the death through the existing authoritative dead state in the same transaction and add no seventh level. Immunity to the Exhaustion condition (PDF183) means a gain does not affect the creature at all: no level, no penalty, no history of a hidden level.

D20 Tests (PDF6–7, 181): every ability check, attack roll and saving throw the creature makes has its roll reduced by 2 times its current level. This includes Initiative and Death Saving Throws, which are an ability check and a saving throw (PDF13, 17). The reduction is a numeric term recorded in the #61 dice receipt beside the natural die; it is not Disadvantage, and it combines with Advantage, Disadvantage and other modifiers without replacing them. Natural-20 and natural-1 results that the source ties to the number on the d20 (attack hit or miss on PDF7, Death Saving Throw results on PDF18) keep using the die face. Damage rolls, DCs the creature sets, AC and passive scores are not D20 Tests and get no invented reduction.

Speed (PDF181, 188): the creature's Speed is reduced by 5 feet times its current level, and each special speed it has is reduced by the same amount. The reduction feeds the one authoritative current Speed that Dash #780 and righting from Prone (`edda-270:prone-movement`) read. A Speed that is 0 and can't increase stays 0. A reduction larger than the remaining Speed yields 0 movement, never a negative stored Speed; this clamp is an Edda adaptation, not source text.

Removal (PDF181, 185): a completed Long Rest removes 1 level; when the level reaches 0 the condition ends and both penalties disappear. Reduce-one removes exactly one removable level, never below 0 and never several levels unless a qualified source removes more. Levels differ only in provenance; taking the most recently gained removable level first is an Edda ordering, not source text. A source owner may mark its own levels non-removable until its stated fact is met (PDF181 dehydration, PDF185 malnutrition) or remove all levels of its own source (PDF189 suffocation); this task supplies only that provenance and predicate, using isolated trusted facts, and never infers a meal, drink or breath. Revival (PDF180): when a trusted qualified revival receipt returns a creature that died with any levels, it returns with 1 fewer level; a creature that died at level 6 returns at 5. Revival itself, its Hit Points and its other consequences stay with #618 and #947.

## Deterministic fixtures

- Two trusted gains from different sources give level 2, not a single flag: natural 14 with +3 becomes 17 − 4 = 13, and Speed 30 becomes 20 with Climb Speed 30 also 20. Level 3: Speed 15, and righting from Prone through the Prone movement task costs 7. Level 5: −10 and Speed 5.
- A sixth level kills the creature once; replay does not kill or add again. A seventh level, a client-declared level, a multi-level gain without a qualified source, a negative level or a gain on an immune creature rejects without mutation.
- Level 1, attack roll natural 20: the reduction is recorded and the attack still hits; natural 1 still misses. Level 1 Death Saving Throw natural 11 becomes 9 and fails; natural 20 still regains 1 Hit Point. Level 2 Initiative is reduced by 4. Reject treating the penalty as Disadvantage, applying it to a damage roll, save DC, AC or passive score, or applying it twice with Advantage.
- Speed 20 at level 5 has 0 movement and cannot right itself; Speed 0 from actual Grappled #778 stays 0 at every level and returns to the reduced value when the grapple ends.
- A trusted reduce-one at level 1 ends the condition and restores the unreduced roll and Speed; at level 0 it is a no-op without history. With one non-removable level and one ordinary level, reduce-one removes the ordinary level; with only the non-removable level it removes nothing. Remove-all-of-source removes only that source's levels.
- Died at level 2, revived by an isolated trusted revival receipt: level 1. Died at level 6: level 5. A forged, foreign or replayed revival receipt changes nothing.

## Durable implementation acceptance

Preview is inert: no dice, writes or hidden facts. Confirm revalidates campaign, actor, trusted source and receipt, immunity, current level, source version and expected revision. Commit the level change, provenance, derived roll and Speed projection, any death and public history atomically. A penalty applied inside another owner's roll belongs to that roll's receipt; this task adds no optional activation. Inject a failure after each stage and prove full isolated PostgreSQL rollback. Same action ID and payload replay after retry, reload or reconnect returns the saved receipt with no second level, death or reroll; a conflicting payload rejects; racing gains or reductions each commit once in a serial order. Web and trusted TUI show the player's own level and both penalties, share unavailable reasons and confirm/cancel/reconnect behavior. Changed web requires focused build/lint and desktop/phone checks. An NPC's level, sources and private rolls never enter previews, receipts, errors or public history. Preserve legacy reads and unrelated JSON: the legacy `exhausted` combat flag and player status carry no level and are never converted into one.

Hard prerequisites: #61 dice receipts, #64 confirmation, #66 turn and action economy, and the Prone movement task published with this decision (`edda-270:prone-movement`). Parent: #270. Directly block #623, the existing direct #279 and #280 blocker that removes one level and recomputes these penalties; that single edge makes this task mandatory for #279, #280 and, through #551 and #1001, #337. #279 is at its 100-blocker cap: add no direct #279 edge. Add no #67 or #429 consumer, no edge to #428 or #1001, and change no existing body, parent or other prerequisite.
