Implement two record-only creation choices.

Languages: every player character knows Common. The player also gains two languages from the Standard Languages table, each chosen or rolled on 1d12: 1 Common Sign Language, 2 Draconic, 3–4 Dwarvish, 5–6 Elvish, 7 Giant, 8 Gnomish, 9 Goblin, 10–11 Halfling, 12 Orc. Save each injected roll. The saved result is Common plus two distinct standard languages. The source does not say how a repeated roll resolves, so a roll that repeats an already known language is saved and shown, and the player must explicitly roll again or choose; nothing is replaced silently. Rare languages (Abyssal, Celestial, Deep Speech, Druidic, Infernal, Primordial, Sylvan, Thieves' Cant, Undercommon) are never offered by this step; only a named feature grants one, through its own owner (#640 Druidic, #1105 Thieves' Cant, #987 Deft Explorer). Primordial's Aquan, Auran, Ignan and Terran dialects are one language record. Knowing a language records speak/read/write and executes nothing else. Language provenance follows #430.

Alignment: record exactly one of Lawful Good, Neutral Good, Chaotic Good, Lawful Neutral, Neutral, Chaotic Neutral, Lawful Evil, Neutral Evil, Chaotic Evil. The three evil alignments require an authoritative GM approval fact, because the source tells the player to check with the GM first. Unaligned is not a player-character option. Alignment is descriptive: it changes no number, eligibility or effect. Effects that read a creature's alignment keep their own owners.

Positive fixtures: chosen Dwarvish and Elvish give Common, Dwarvish, Elvish; injected d12 4 and 11 give Dwarvish and Halfling; injected 10 then 11 repeats Halfling and waits for an explicit new roll or choice; Neutral Good records with no approval; Lawful Evil records only with the GM fact. Reject: one or three extra languages; Common chosen as an extra; a rare language; a d12 result outside 1–12 or a row mismatch; a tenth alignment or Unaligned; an evil alignment without approval; a second alignment; any derived bonus from either record.

Preview is inert: no dice, writes or history. Confirm revalidates campaign, actor, player/GM authority, source version, every choice and the expected revision, then persists choices, injected dice, grants, resource deltas, derived sheet and public history atomically. Fail at each stage and prove full isolated PostgreSQL rollback. Same-ID retry/reload/reconnect returns the saved receipt without reroll, regrant or recharge; a conflicting payload rejects and concurrent confirmations commit once. Shared web and trusted TUI expose only qualified choices and explicit unavailable reasons, with accessible cancel/confirm/reconnect and desktop/phone checks for changed web. Keep private GM facts, hidden actors and private choices out of discovery, preview, errors and public history. Tests use explicit isolated entitlements and grant no production admission.
