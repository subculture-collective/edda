- Giant Ancestry: choose exactly one of six benefits at creation: Cloud's Jaunt, Fire's Burn, Frost's Chill, Hill's Tumble, Stone's Endurance or Storm's Thunder. The chosen benefit has uses equal to the Proficiency Bonus and all return on a completed Long Rest. This task owns the choice, the use pool and its recovery for all six, and the three on-hit benefits:
  - Fire's Burn: on hitting a target with an attack roll and dealing damage to it, also deal 1d10 Fire damage to that target.
  - Frost's Chill: on such a hit, also deal 1d6 Cold damage and reduce the target's Speed by 10 feet until the start of the Goliath's next turn.
  - Hill's Tumble: on such a hit against a Large or smaller creature, give that target the Prone condition.
- Powerful Build: Advantage on any ability check made to end the Grappled condition, and the Goliath counts as one size larger when determining carrying capacity.

Cloud's Jaunt, Stone's Endurance and Storm's Thunder execute only in the #333 Goliath jaunt and reaction task, and Large Form only in the #333 Goliath Large Form task. Until the jaunt and reaction task lands, its three benefits are listed at creation with an explicit unavailable reason and cannot be chosen. Consume #65 for the hit and damage receipt the on-hit benefits attach to, #66 for the turn boundary that ends the Speed reduction, #61 for check Advantage, #488 for Prone through the #333 condition output, #778 for the Grappled escape check and #428 for recovery. On-hit benefits are optional, spend one use each and are declared on the confirmed hit, never retroactively. No published issue owns carrying capacity or a timed Speed reduction, so both are consumed here in a bounded way and neither becomes a general framework: carrying capacity is the PDF178 table for the Goliath's own sheet, where a Medium Goliath uses the Large row (Strength×30 lb. carry, Strength×60 lb. drag, lift or push); the Speed reduction is one named effect on the target that ends at the start of the Goliath's next turn. Target size is an authoritative scene fact of #67's closed contract; an unknown size keeps Hill's Tumble unavailable.
