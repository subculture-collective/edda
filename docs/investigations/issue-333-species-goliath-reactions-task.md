This task executes the three Giant Ancestry benefits that are not on-hit riders. The ancestry choice, its use pool equal to the Proficiency Bonus and its Long Rest recovery belong to the #333 Goliath task; each benefit here spends one use from that pool and adds no pool of its own.

- Cloud's Jaunt: as a Bonus Action, magically teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space the Goliath can see.
- Stone's Endurance: when the Goliath takes damage, a Reaction rolls 1d12, adds the Constitution modifier and reduces the damage by that total.
- Storm's Thunder: when the Goliath takes damage from a creature within 60 feet, a Reaction deals 1d8 Thunder damage to that creature.

Consume #66 for the Bonus Action and #81 for the Reaction window opened by the damage event. Stone's Endurance is offered inside the damage event before Hit Points change, and the reduction is a penalty to that damage applied before any Resistance, as PDF17 orders; the damage cannot fall below0. Storm's Thunder is offered after the triggering damage is known and has no attack roll or save. No published issue owns teleportation as movement, so it is consumed here in a bounded way from PDF190: the Goliath disappears and reappears without crossing the space between, spends no movement and provokes no Opportunity Attack; equipment worn and carried goes with it and nothing else does. A visible unoccupied destination, distance and the damaging creature's identity and range are authoritative scene facts of #67's closed contract; unknown facts keep the benefit unavailable.

Positive fixtures: Cloud's Jaunt moves the Goliath to a declared visible unoccupied space at30 feet, spends one use and the Bonus Action and no movement, and a declared adjacent enemy gets no Opportunity Attack. Stone's Endurance injected7 with CON+2 reduces9 damage to0 and12 damage to3. Storm's Thunder injected5 deals5 Thunder to an attacker at60 feet. Reject: a benefit the Goliath did not choose; a use beyond the pool; Cloud's Jaunt to an occupied or unseen space, beyond30 feet or without a Bonus Action; a Reaction when none is available or two Reactions to one damage event; Stone's Endurance reducing damage below0, healing, or declared after the damage is committed; Storm's Thunder against a creature at65 feet, against damage with no creature source or with an attack roll; a carried creature teleported.
