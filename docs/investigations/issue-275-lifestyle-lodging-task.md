## Bounded outcome

Implement the periodic lifestyle choice and the seventeen Food, Drink, and Lodging purchases. Every charge uses the holding and payment from the `edda-275:coin-holdings-payment` task; implement no second ledger or payment.

Lifestyle (PDF101): Wretched is free; Squalid costs 1 SP per day, Poor 2 SP, Modest 1 GP, Comfortable 2 GP, Wealthy 4 GP and Aristocratic 10 GP. At the start of each week or month, whichever the GM chose for the campaign, the character chooses one lifestyle and pays its price for that period. The source defines neither the length of a week nor of a month, and no issue owns campaign time. This PR therefore adds one GM-recorded lifestyle period per campaign: unit (week or month), day count, opened and closed by explicit GM action. No clock advances it. The charge is the daily price times that day count. The expense covers lodging, food, equipment maintenance and other necessities. Lifestyles have no inherent consequences: add no modifier, condition, rest change, risk roll, reputation score or equipment decay. The GM may take the recorded lifestyle into account; that stays a GM judgment. The source states no mid-period change, refund, arrears or automatic downgrade, so none exists here. It also states no consequence for being unable to pay. Edda adaptation: a choice the holding cannot cover is rejected and no lifestyle is recorded for that period.

Food, Drink, and Lodging (PDF101–102), each a single listed-price purchase: Ale (mug) 4 CP; Bread (loaf) 2 CP; Cheese (wedge) 1 SP; Inn Stay per Day at Squalid 7 CP, Poor 1 SP, Modest 5 SP, Comfortable 8 SP, Wealthy 2 GP, Aristocratic 4 GP; Meal at Squalid 1 CP, Poor 2 CP, Modest 1 SP, Comfortable 2 SP, Wealthy 3 SP, Aristocratic 6 SP; Wine (bottle) Common 2 SP, Fine 10 GP. There is no Wretched row. An inn stay is one night's lodging and grants no rest, healing or safety by itself: Short and Long Rests stay with #427 and #428. The table gives no weight for a meal or loaf, so a purchase does not by itself satisfy or change the PDF185 Malnutrition or PDF181 Dehydration hazards. Rations and other gear keep their own Equipment rows.

Inclusion (PDF101). The source says only: "The Food, Drink, and Lodging table gives prices for food and a single night's lodging. Prices for daily lodging and meals are included in your lifestyle's expenses." It does not say which table row a lifestyle includes, how many meals a day, or what a different grade costs a character who has already paid. Edda adaptation: inclusion is a GM-recorded fact and nothing is included automatically. During a character's recorded period the GM may mark one specific Inn Stay per Day or Meal purchase by that character as included; that purchase charges nothing and its receipt names the period and the mark. No lifestyle is mapped to a table row and no daily quantity is inferred. Without a mark, every row is bought at its table price.

## Deterministic fixtures

- With a GM-opened period of unit week and 7 days, Modest costs 7 GP and Squalid 7 SP. With a period of unit month and 30 days, Poor costs 60 SP (6 GP) and Aristocratic 300 GP. Wretched records the choice and charges nothing.
- A second choice inside the same paid period, a choice with no open GM period, a period with no day count, a refund request, a lifestyle name outside the seven, or a holding that cannot cover the price rejects without partial changes. No debt, downgrade or lifestyle record is created.
- Under a paid Modest period, a Modest inn stay the GM marked as included charges nothing. The same stay without a mark costs 5 SP, a Modest meal without a mark 1 SP, a Wealthy inn stay 2 GP and a mug of Ale 4 CP. A mark with no recorded period for that character, a mark on any row other than Inn Stay per Day or Meal, and a mark by anyone but the GM reject.
- Fine Wine costs 10 GP and Common Wine 2 SP. Reject a Wretched inn stay or meal, an invented row, a negative or fractional quantity and a marked purchase charged or granted twice on replay.
- A recorded Aristocratic or Wretched lifestyle leaves every d20 Test, condition, rest and Hit Point unchanged.
