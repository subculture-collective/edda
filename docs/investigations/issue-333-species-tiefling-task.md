This task owns only the species identity, its nested choices and the traits named here. Class creation and level applications consume its output and apply each grant once; it grants no class, background or feat. The creation provenance contract stays with #430 and is consumed through the #333 creation provenance output. Tiefling is Humanoid, Speed30.

- Size: Medium or Small, chosen when the species is selected and fixed afterwards.
- Darkvision 60 feet.
- Fiendish Legacy: choose Abyssal, Chthonic or Infernal at creation, and at the same time choose Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma as the spellcasting ability for this trait's spells.
  - Abyssal: Resistance to Poison damage; knows Poison Spray. Level3 Ray of Sickness; level5 Hold Person.
  - Chthonic: Resistance to Necrotic damage; knows Chill Touch. Level3 False Life; level5 Ray of Enfeeblement.
  - Infernal: Resistance to Fire damage; knows Fire Bolt. Level3 Hellish Rebuke; level5 Darkness.
  The level3 and level5 spells are gained at those character levels and are always prepared. Each can be cast once without a spell slot, regained on a completed Long Rest, and can also be cast with any slot of the appropriate level the character has.
- Otherworldly Presence: knows Thaumaturgy; cast through this trait it uses the same ability chosen for Fiendish Legacy.

This task owns eligibility, the per-spell free use and its recovery. Slot versus free-use routing stays with #1448. Each spell executes only through its own effect owner: Poison Spray #1075, Ray of Sickness #695, Hold Person #683, Chill Touch #1264, False Life #1318, Ray of Enfeeblement #1631/#1632, Fire Bolt #678, Hellish Rebuke #1607, Darkness #1282 and Thaumaturgy #959. Every one of those owners is a hard prerequisite, so no legacy grant can close unexecuted. The legacy's Resistance is projected only through the #333 typed Resistance task (PDF17 and187: halved, rounded down, once per damage instance); this task registers the trait as one named source and adds no halving or resistance store of its own. Consume #145 for Darkvision, #428 for recovery and #968 for the level3/5 thresholds.

Positive fixtures: Infernal level1 takes4 from9 Fire damage through the typed Resistance task and knows Fire Bolt with the chosen ability. At level3 Hellish Rebuke is cast once without a slot in its own Reaction window, then only with a level1 or higher slot until a completed Long Rest. At level5 Darkness has a separate free use and afterwards needs a level2 or higher slot. Thaumaturgy uses the legacy ability, not a class ability. Small Tiefling records size Small. Reject: no legacy or two; ability outside the three, changed later or different for Thaumaturgy; level3 spell at level2; one shared free use across two spells; free use restored by a Short Rest; a legacy spell cast with a slot below its level; a cantrip or resistance from another legacy; a halving applied inside this task; no size or a later size change.
