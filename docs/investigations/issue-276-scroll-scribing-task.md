## Bounded outcome

Implement only "Scribing Spell Scrolls" on PDF103 for one single-class Cleric scribe and three named spells from #217: Sacred Flame (cantrip), Sanctuary (level 1) and Revivify (level 3). The created item is the canonical Spell Scroll identity that #1142 validates. Its records are #262 for the PDF99 Spell Scroll (Cantrip) and Spell Scroll (Level 1), and #242 for the PDF244 Spell Scroll family that the level 3 scroll needs. This task owns the scribing project and the scribe values saved on the created scroll. It adds no spell effect and no class grant. Reading a scribed scroll with its saved values belongs to the #276 scribed scroll reading task (marker `edda-276:scribed-scroll-reading`).

Time and cost (PDF103, all ten rows retained as data): cantrip 1 day 15 GP; level 1 1 day 25 GP; level 2 3 days 100 GP; level 3 5 days 150 GP; level 4 10 days 1,000 GP; level 5 25 days 1,500 GP; level 6 40 days 10,000 GP; level 7 50 days 12,500 GP; level 8 60 days 15,000 GP; level 9 120 days 50,000 GP. Each day of inscription is 8 hours of work; days need not be consecutive. Only the cantrip, level 1 and level 3 rows execute. Levels 2 and 4–9, other classes' preparation and multiclass scribes belong to the #276 scribing coverage spike (marker `edda-276:scribing-coverage-spike`) and stay unavailable with that reason. PDF207 keeps Spell Scrolls out of the magic-item consumable halving. The scribing rule names no assistants and no raw-material availability ruling; add neither. Reuse the #276 crafting project ledger task (marker `edda-276:crafting-project-ledger`).

Prerequisites for the scribe (PDF103): "you must have proficiency in the Arcana skill or with Calligrapher's Supplies and have the spell prepared on each day of the inscription." Use the actual Cleric preparation output #557. A day on which the spell is not prepared cannot be recorded. A cantrip is known, not prepared; accept it only when it is among the scribe's own qualified Cleric cantrips on that day (Edda reading of the Cantrips clause).

Calligrapher's Supplies: PDF93 lists Spell Scroll under that tool's Craft entry, and PDF103 names only proficiency. The source does not say whether a scribe who qualifies through the supplies must hold them. A scribe with Arcana proficiency needs no tool. A scribe who qualifies only through Calligrapher's Supplies proficiency must have the supplies in inventory, or a recorded GM ruling; this is an Edda conservative bound.

Material components (PDF103): "You must also have at hand any Material components required by the spell; if the spell consumes its Material components, they are consumed only when you complete the scroll." The source ties "each day" to preparation only and gives no timing for the components. The one fixed point is completion: require every component in the completing transaction and consume a consumed component there and nowhere else. A workday other than the last on which a component is absent is unavailable without a recorded GM ruling; this is an Edda conservative bound, not SRD text. The source does not say a Component Pouch or Spellcasting Focus can stand in for a component during scribing, so neither does. Validate and consume owned priced materials through the #1447 material model; #618 owns the Revivify diamond check. #1447's cast boundary does not list Sanctuary or Revivify, so reuse its ownership validation only and no cast admission.

Scribe values (PDF103): "The scroll's spell uses your spell save DC and spell attack bonus." For a cantrip, "the version on the scroll works as if the caster were your level": save the scribe's total character level, the quantity cantrip upgrades use (#957 scales Sacred Flame by total character level). Save these values on the created scroll instance at completion, with the class that supplied the preparation. They never change afterwards. PDF99 and PDF244 give fixed values by spell level; #1142 implements those and its body, parent and edges stay untouched.

The scroll bears the spell at its own level. The source gives no rule for scribing at a higher level, from an item, from a ritual book entry or from another caster's preparation; all stay unavailable. Copying a scroll into a spellbook remains #1770.

## Deterministic fixtures

- A level-5 Cleric with Arcana proficiency, Sanctuary prepared and a mirror shard in inventory: 1 day, 25 GP, one level-1 scroll saving the scribe's own DC and attack bonus (for WIS +3 and PB +3: DC 14, +6). The shard is not consumed.
- The same Cleric with Calligrapher's Supplies proficiency and the supplies, without Arcana, scribes Sacred Flame: 1 day, 15 GP, saved caster level 5.
- Revivify: 5 days, 150 GP, a diamond worth 300+ GP. Record days 1–3, unprepare the spell, and day 4 rejects; prepare it again and days 4–5 complete. The diamond leaves inventory only in the completing transaction, and day 5 rejects without it.
- Reject independently: neither proficiency; Calligrapher's Supplies proficiency without the supplies and without a ruling; spell not prepared; another class's or another character's preparation; a diamond worth 299 GP; a Component Pouch for the shard; a component absent on an earlier day without a ruling; diamond consumed at start; 24 GP for level 1; a sixth day; an assistant dividing the time; a level-2 scroll of Sanctuary; a level-2 or level-4 spell, with the coverage spike named as the reason; a multiclass scribe; an unknown spell identity; rewriting saved values after the scribe levels up.
- Reject an invented Arcana check, failure or mishap roll, ink or parchment consumption, slot expenditure or sale.
