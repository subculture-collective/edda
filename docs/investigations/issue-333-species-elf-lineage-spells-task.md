Source: English SRD 5.2.1 PDF84–85 (Elven Lineage; level3 and level5 rows of the Elven Lineages table) and the Wizard cantrip list on79; SHA-256 `8974902d109d6e63672d7c490bde9ccf052410503d9cfa768237154fbc5e3d87`. This task owns only what is named here, on top of the lineage and spellcasting ability recorded by the #333 Elf task. It grants no class, background or feat.

- At character level3 and again at level5 the Elf learns the spell its lineage lists and always has it prepared: High Elf Detect Magic then Misty Step; Wood Elf Longstrider then Pass without Trace; Drow Faerie Fire then Darkness.
- Each such spell can be cast once without a spell slot, regained on a completed Long Rest, and can also be cast with any spell slot of the appropriate level the character has. Every spell has its own free use.
- High Elf only: whenever the Elf finishes a Long Rest it may replace the Prestidigitation cantrip with a different cantrip from the Wizard spell list. The source speaks only of replacing "that cantrip"; as an Edda decision the lineage holds exactly one such cantrip at a time, and the current one is what a later Long Rest may replace.

This task owns eligibility, the per-spell free use and its recovery, and the replacement choice. Slot versus free-use routing stays with #1448. Each spell executes only through its own effect owner: Detect Magic #265/#906, Misty Step #688, Longstrider #1066, Pass without Trace #1072, Darkness #1282. Faerie Fire has no published effect owner, so the Drow level3 grant, its free use and its cast are owned by the #333 Faerie Fire task; until that task lands a level3 Drow sees Faerie Fire with an explicit unavailable reason and nothing else in this task depends on it. Consume #428 for Long Rest completion and #968 for the level3/5 thresholds.

The Wizard cantrip list on PDF79 is Acid Splash, Chill Touch, Dancing Lights, Elementalism, Fire Bolt, Light, Mage Hand, Mending, Message, Minor Illusion, Poison Spray, Prestidigitation, Ray of Frost, Shocking Grasp and True Strike. The replacement offers a cantrip only while that cantrip's own effect owner is qualified, and lists every other one with an explicit unavailable reason; making a cantrip's effect work is that owner's obligation, not this task's. This task cannot close on an empty offer: Dancing Lights #1281 is required through the #333 Elf task, so the replacement must work with it. The Wizard class's own cantrip replacement stays with #1766.

Positive fixtures: High Elf level3 casts Detect Magic once without a slot, then only with a level1 or higher slot until a completed Long Rest. At level5 Misty Step has its own separate free use and afterwards needs a level2 or higher slot. Wood Elf level3 casts Longstrider free once; at level5 Pass without Trace needs a level2 or higher slot after its free use. Drow level5 casts Darkness free once. A High Elf finishing a Long Rest replaces Prestidigitation with Dancing Lights, casts it with the lineage ability, and after the next Long Rest may replace Dancing Lights. Reject: a level3 spell at level2 or a level5 spell at level4; one shared free use across two spells; a free use restored by a Short Rest; a lineage spell cast with a slot below its level; another lineage's spell; a replacement cantrip from another list, the same cantrip, a second replacement in one rest, a replacement outside a completed Long Rest or by a non-High Elf; a replacement whose effect owner is unqualified; a Faerie Fire cast through this task.
