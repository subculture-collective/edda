- Darkvision 120 feet.
- Dwarven Resilience: Resistance to Poison damage, and Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
- Dwarven Toughness: Hit Point maximum +1 at creation and +1 again whenever the character gains a level, whatever class the level is in.
- Stonecunning: a Bonus Action gives Tremorsense 60 feet for 10 minutes. The Dwarf must be on a stone surface or touching one, natural or worked, to use the Tremorsense. Uses equal the Proficiency Bonus; all return on a completed Long Rest.
