# Interrupted rest, recovery and recharge ownership (#272)

Most named rest and recharge rules already have bounded published owners: #427 for the Short Rest, #428 for the Long Rest and #1148 for one wand's dawn recharge. Reuse them without changing their bodies. Whole-body inspection of all 1,892 issues found five source clauses with no owner. This decision assigns them to six implementation tasks and adds one qualification task that carries all of them to the item gate. The seven exact bodies are the companion task files; native publication, numbers and readback are recorded later in the link-verification receipt. Closing this decision supplies no runtime.

## Unowned residuals

1. **Knocking Out a Creature (PDF17, 184).** A melee attack that would reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points can leave it at 1, Unconscious and starting a Short Rest. No issue owns it; Rogue #1120 is a different Cunning Strike effect. The task consumes #427 and #487 and names the three source endings. After an interrupted rest the source gives no further ending, so none is invented. The closed healing fixtures #263 and #266 admit only a conscious ally, so the healing ending is tested with an injected committed event and has no player-facing path until a healing owner such as #615 admits an Unconscious target.
2. **Temporary Hit Points (PDF18, 190).** Sixteen published tasks grant them: #573, #645, #698, #724, #725, #807, #912, #1000, #1318, #1497, #1638–#1642 and #1969. Each keeps its own per-source non-stacking and duration fixture, and #725 refers to a shared rule that no issue supplies. No body states the damage order with carry-over, that healing cannot restore the pool, the keep-or-replace choice across sources, or removal of the pool when #428 completes. The task owns only those clauses. It is a downstream consolidation with False Life #1318 as its witness, because existing edges cannot change. The open splitter #358 expects to reuse #272's Temporary Hit Point owner for Heroism and must name this task as a prerequisite.
3. **Sleep during a Long Rest (PDF185, 191).** #428 requires 6 hours of sleep but does not state that the sleeper is Unconscious. The task binds each recorded sleep interval to one #487 instance. The Long Rest and Unconscious entries name no waking trigger, so waking requires an explicit trusted fact. Source-specific wakers keep their owners and supply that fact: the Alarm ping #1035 (PDF107) and the Weapon of Warning (PDF253), which stays with #278.
4. **Wand of Magic Missiles cast and last-charge destruction (PDF206, 251).** #1140 and #1148 are limited to a wand with no destruction clause, and no other issue rolls for a last charge. This wand is the smallest witness: no Attunement, 1 to 3 charges for levels 1 to 3 and a d20 on the last charge. The actual darts stay with #1368. Its 1d6 + 1 dawn recharge is a second task, following the #1140 and #1148 split. Both tasks treat #1140 and #1148 as build-order prerequisites whose charge record and dawn event they extend without widening the item domain, so both wait on #906 and the Rogue execution cohort #1097. PDF206 names two ways to learn a charge count, Identify and Attunement; that a holder with neither learns nothing is an Edda disclosure adaptation, kept as a negative fixture. The item key `srd-5.2.1:content/magic-items/wand-of-magic-missiles` is assigned here, so #278 must not publish a second owner.
5. **Hit Point maximum of 0 (PDF17).** A creature dies if its maximum reaches 0. No body states it; the reducers #1705 and #1709 only lower the maximum. One task owns the death predicate with Life Drain #1705 as its witness, and records that a rest never restores a creature killed this way.

Wider scope keeps its existing planning owner and is not executable:

- #278: all other charged or dawn items, item resilience, Artifact destruction, sentient-item limits, attuned charge knowledge and the Weapon of Warning.
- #333: Elf Trance.
- #150, which names food, water and suffocation: Exhaustion from dehydration cannot be removed until the creature drinks a full day's water (PDF181), Exhaustion from malnutrition cannot be removed until it eats a full day's food (PDF185), and a creature that can breathe again removes all Exhaustion gained from suffocating (PDF189). #428 cannot be qualified against hazard-sourced Exhaustion until those children exist; the qualification task carries a contrary control for it.
- #270: Exhaustion effects.
- #182: Recharge X–Y, including its Short or Long Rest recharge, and Recharge after a Short or Long Rest (PDF257).

Outside the five named scopes and without a published owner: Death Saving Throws, stabilizing a creature and the rule that a Stable creature regains 1 Hit Point after 1d4 hours (PDF17–18). Legacy combat code tracks death saves; Spare the Dying #958 defers Stable to #487, whose body does not mention it. Those pages are in the source range of world qualification #183 only. This decision does not assign them and the knock-out task does not use them; they need an owner decision.
