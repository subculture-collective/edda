This task executes Large Form for a character recorded as a Goliath by the #333 Goliath task. It adds nothing to Giant Ancestry.

- Large Form, starting at character level5: as a Bonus Action the Goliath changes its size to Large if it is in a big enough space. The transformation lasts 10 minutes or until the Goliath ends it (no action required). For that duration it has Advantage on Strength checks and its Speed increases by 10 feet. Once used, the trait cannot be used again until a completed Long Rest.

Consume #66 for the Bonus Action, #61 for check Advantage and #968 for the level5 threshold; Long Rest completion is #428's, required through the #333 Goliath task. While Large, Powerful Build still counts the Goliath as one size larger for carrying capacity, so the PDF178 Huge row applies (Strength×60 lb. carry, Strength×120 lb. drag, lift or push) and the Large row returns when the form ends. Whether the space is big enough for a Large creature is an authoritative scene fact of #67's closed contract; an unknown space keeps the trait unavailable. The source says nothing about worn equipment or about the form ending in a space that has become too small, so this task changes no equipment and adds no forced ending beyond the three the source names.

Positive fixtures: at level5 in a declared big enough space the Goliath becomes Large, Speed35 becomes45, a Strength check rolls with Advantage and Strength16 carries960 lb. Ending it with no action, or 10 minutes passing, restores Medium, Speed35 and480 lb. A completed Long Rest makes it available again. Reject: Large Form at level4; in a declared too-small or unknown space; as an action other than a Bonus Action; a second use before a completed Long Rest or restored by a Short Rest; Advantage on Strength saving throws or attack rolls; a Speed bonus or size that outlasts the form; a non-Goliath.
