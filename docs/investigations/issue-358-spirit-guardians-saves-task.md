## Bounded outcome

Implement only one source identity: the Spirit Guardians effect, for one isolated, explicitly entitled Cleric fixture. This task owns the creatures designated as unaffected, membership of the caster's 15-foot Emanation, the three source triggers, the once-per-turn Wisdom save and its damage. It does not own the Speed halving (#2059), Cleric casting modes, any other spell, a generic area or start-of-turn scheduler, a same-moment ordering choice, monster spellcasting or production Cleric eligibility.

Cast (PDF164): the fixture's explicit isolated entitlement supplies the committed cast operands: the caster, the Magic action, range Self, the spent slot level (three or higher), the bound spell save DC, the caster's alignment and the cast ID. Spend the slot and start Concentration, up to 10 minutes, through the closed slot-casting and Concentration baselines #68 and #77; starting it ends the caster's earlier Concentration through the existing owner. This task does not validate V, S or M, the prayer scroll, a Component Pouch or Spellcasting Focus in its place, the free hand, the one-slot-per-turn rule or the DC derivation (PDF105–106). Those stay with Cleric casting #556, and no production cast exists until it qualifies. A slot below level three or a caster without the entitlement is rejected. The cosmetic angelic, fey or fiendish form has no mechanical effect.

Designation (PDF164): only when the spell is cast, the caster may designate creatures as unaffected. The source states no number, sight or knowledge limit. As a labelled Edda disclosure adaptation, only creatures already known to the caster's player can be named, because a choice list must not reveal a hidden creature; a hidden creature is therefore affected. Persist the set with the cast. It cannot be extended, reduced or re-chosen later. A creature not designated at cast time is affected, including one that arrives later.

Membership (PDF177, 181): the Emanation extends 15 feet from the caster and moves with the caster. Its origin is not in the area unless its creator decides otherwise. This PR does not offer that choice: including the caster stays unavailable with an explicit reason, as a labelled Edda restriction, so the caster never saves. A creature is inside when its trusted exact distance to the caster is 15 feet or less and no Total Cover blocks every straight line from the caster to it. Unknown distance, sight or cover facts keep the affected action or turn transition unavailable with an explicit reason; never guess a position.

Triggers (PDF164–165): a non-designated creature makes a Wisdom saving throw against the bound spell save DC whenever (1) the caster's movement brings the Emanation onto the creature, (2) the creature enters the Emanation, or (3) the creature ends its own turn inside it. Nothing else triggers the save: not the cast itself for a creature already inside, not the start of any turn, not the caster's turn end, and not staying inside during another creature's turn. On a failed save the creature takes 3d8 damage; on a success, half as much, rounded down (PDF5, 16). Each slot level above three adds 1d8. When one movement step forces two or more creatures to save at the same time, roll the damage once for all of them (PDF16).

Damage type (PDF21–22, 165): Radiant when the caster is good or neutral, Necrotic when the caster is evil. The source states no type for an unaligned caster, so an unaligned or unknown alignment keeps the cast unavailable. No published issue owns a player character's alignment fact. The fixture supplies it explicitly, and a production cast stays unavailable until such an owner exists.

Once per turn (PDF165): a creature makes this save at most once per turn. Bind the save to the cast ID, the creature ID and the identity of the turn in progress (round, turn owner and turn sequence), not to a round or to wall time. The same creature may save again on a later turn in the same round.

Timing identity: every trigger is a consequence of a committed movement or end-turn action. Preview of that action lists the saves it will force without rolling. Confirm commits the movement or turn end, the membership revision, the save, the damage and the dedup record together. No optional activation is invented for the caster, and no background tick runs between actions.

Simultaneous events (PDF187): when another effect would resolve at the same moment for the same creature, the person whose turn it is decides the order. This PR builds no ordering choice and no scheduler. A movement or turn end that would resolve this save together with another same-moment effect on that creature stays unavailable with an explicit reason until an ordering owner exists. Never default an order.

Overlap (PDF106): the effects of the same spell cast more than once do not combine, and the most potent or most recent cast applies. This PR supports one active Spirit Guardians in an encounter. A second concurrent cast in the same encounter stays unavailable with an explicit reason; general potency ordering remains with residual reconciliation #277.

Ending (PDF179): the Emanation, membership records and pending triggers end with the caster's Concentration through #353 (a failed Constitution save after damage, Incapacitated, death, a new Concentration effect, voluntary end) or at ten minutes. Ending never refunds the slot and never reverses committed damage.

## Deterministic fixtures

- Level-five Cleric entitlement (PDF36: proficiency bonus +3, a level-three slot), WIS +3, good alignment, bound DC 14 (8 + 3 + 3), injected 3d8 = 4, 5, 6: a failed save takes 15 Radiant and a successful save takes 7. An evil caster deals Necrotic.
- Level-seven Cleric entitlement (PDF36: proficiency bonus +3, a level-four slot), bound DC 14, injected 4d8 = 4, 5, 6, 3: a failed save takes 18 and a successful save takes 9. The level-five entitlement cannot spend a level-four slot.
- A level-two slot, a caster without the explicit entitlement and a client-supplied DC, damage type or dice are rejected without mutation. An unaligned or unknown alignment is unavailable with its reason.
- Caster moves from 20 feet to 15 feet of an opponent on the caster's turn: one save on that turn. Stepping away and back on the same turn forces no second save. On the opponent's own turn it stays and ends its turn inside: a second save, on a different turn.
- An opponent 20 feet away moves to 15 feet on its own turn and ends its turn inside: exactly one save, at entry, and none at turn end. An opponent that starts its turn inside and never leaves saves only at its turn end, not at its turn start.
- An opponent already at 10 feet when the spell is cast does not save at the cast. At exactly 15 feet a creature is inside; at 20 feet it is outside. Total Cover from the caster keeps a creature within 15 feet outside the area.
- A creature designated at the cast never saves and takes no damage at any trigger. A request to designate a creature after the cast or to trigger a save at the start of a turn is rejected. A request to name a hidden creature is rejected without revealing it. A request to include the caster in the area is unavailable with the labelled-restriction reason.
- Two non-designated creatures brought inside by one caster step share one damage roll and make separate saves. Each has its own dedup record.
- A second Spirit Guardians cast while one is active in the same encounter is unavailable without mutation.
- Concentration ending between two triggers leaves the committed first result intact and forces no further save. Reload between any two steps restores the cast, designation, membership, dedup records and remaining duration exactly.
