- Darkvision 60 feet.
- Gnomish Cunning: Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.
- Gnomish Lineage: choose Forest Gnome or Rock Gnome at creation, and at the same time choose Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma as the spellcasting ability for this trait's spells.
  - Forest Gnome: knows Minor Illusion; always has Speak with Animals prepared, can cast it without a slot a number of times equal to the Proficiency Bonus, regaining all on a completed Long Rest, and can also cast it with any slot the character has.
  - Rock Gnome: knows Mending and Prestidigitation. May spend 10 minutes casting Prestidigitation to create a Tiny clockwork device (AC5, 1HP). At creation choose one Prestidigitation effect; if that effect has options, choose one for the device (ignite or extinguish, never both). The device produces that effect whenever the Gnome or another creature takes a Bonus Action to activate it with a touch. At most three exist at a time; each falls apart 8 hours after creation or when the Gnome dismantles it with a touch as a Utilize action.
