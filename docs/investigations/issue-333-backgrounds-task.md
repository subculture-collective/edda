Implement selection of exactly one of the four SRD backgrounds at creation and its fixed grants. Records come from #306; a record's presence is not execution evidence.

- Acolyte: feat Magic Initiate (Cleric); skills Insight and Religion; tool Calligrapher's Supplies; equipment A Calligrapher's Supplies, Book (prayers), Holy Symbol, Parchment (10 sheets), Robe, 8GP, or B 50GP.
- Criminal: feat Alert; skills Sleight of Hand and Stealth; tool Thieves' Tools; equipment A 2 Daggers, Thieves' Tools, Crowbar, 2 Pouches, Traveler's Clothes, 16GP, or B 50GP.
- Sage: feat Magic Initiate (Wizard); skills Arcana and History; tool Calligrapher's Supplies; equipment A Quarterstaff, Calligrapher's Supplies, Book (history), Parchment (8 sheets), Robe, 8GP, or B 50GP.
- Soldier: feat Savage Attacker; skills Athletics and Intimidation; tool one chosen kind of Gaming Set (dice, dragonchess, playing cards or three-dragon ante); equipment A Spear, Shortbow, 20 Arrows, the same chosen Gaming Set, Healer's Kit, Quiver, Traveler's Clothes, 14GP, or B 50GP.

The feat is granted through #384's durable selection and executes only through its own owner: #385 Alert, #386 Magic Initiate with the list fixed by the background, #387 Savage Attacker. Magic Initiate's remaining nested choices stay in #386. Skills and tool are recorded with background provenance under #430, consumed through the #333 creation provenance output; a duplicate with a class or species grant follows #430 and never doubles the Proficiency Bonus. Equipment is exactly one of A or B, granted once as inventory identities, quantities and coins. Possession executes no item effect; item properties stay with the equipment owners under #278. PDF20 lets coins gained here be spent on equipment at once: this task records the coins, and purchases stay with the price owners under #275. The background ability adjustment belongs to the #333 ability-score task; GM-authored backgrounds belong to the #333 custom-background task. No other background name is admitted.

Positive fixtures: each of the four backgrounds with A and with B gives the exact identities, quantities and coins above; Soldier with dragonchess gives that tool proficiency and, under A, that same set. Reject: mixed A and B; partial A; wrong coin amount; Soldier A with a Gaming Set different from the chosen proficiency; Soldier with no Gaming Set kind or one outside the four variants; Acolyte with Magic Initiate (Wizard) or Sage with Magic Initiate (Cleric); a swapped skill or tool; a second background; an unknown or legacy background name; equipment regrant on replay or on a later class entry.
